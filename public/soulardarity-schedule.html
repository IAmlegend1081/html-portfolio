Soulardarity — IT Work Schedule

Role: IT Coordinator (Phone Consultations, Website, Google Workspace & General IT)
Hours: 20 hrs/week  |  Days: 6 days/week  |  Duration: 2 Weeks

Week 1

Day Tasks Hours
Day 1 — Monday
  • Check and respond to IT support requests from staff
  • Phone consultation: triage incoming tech issues
  • Review Google Workspace alerts & admin dashboard
 3.5 hrs
Day 2 — Tuesday
  • Website maintenance: apply CMS/plugin updates, check for broken links
  • Review and update website content as needed
  • Test site on mobile & desktop; fix any display issues
 3.5 hrs
Day 3 — Wednesday
  • Google Workspace: add/remove users, manage groups & permissions
  • Phone consultation: walk staff through new Google tools or workflows
  • Set up or audit shared Drive folders & access controls
 3.5 hrs
Day 4 — Thursday
  • Hardware/software troubleshooting for staff devices
  • Phone consultation: remote support calls as requested
  • Update antivirus / security software on office machines
 3 hrs
Day 5 — Friday
  • Verify automated data backups completed successfully
  • Security review: check login activity & suspicious alerts
  • Respond to any end-of-week IT tickets
 3 hrs
Day 6 — Saturday
  • Document completed work & outstanding issues
  • Plan Week 2 priorities & schedule phone consultations
  • Draft IT notes / brief for leadership
 3.5 hrs
Week 1 Total 20 hrs

Week 2

Day Tasks Hours
Day 1 — Monday
  • Review IT tickets & incoming support requests
  • Phone consultation: follow-up on Week 1 issues
  • Check Google Workspace — review storage quotas & shared calendars
 3.5 hrs
Day 2 — Tuesday
  • Website: add or edit content (blog posts, event listings, etc.)
  • Check and optimize website performance & uptime monitoring
  • Update SEO metadata / alt text on images
 3.5 hrs
Day 3 — Wednesday
  • Google Workspace: set up email aliases, distribution lists, or forms
  • Phone consultation: staff training on Google Meet / Docs / Sheets
  • Audit third-party app permissions in Google Workspace
 3 hrs
Day 4 — Thursday
  • Network check: review router / Wi-Fi health, update firmware if needed
  • Phone consultation: any newly raised issues from staff
  • Software license review — renewals, unused licenses
 3.5 hrs
Day 5 — Friday
  • Run full backup verification across all critical systems
  • Security audit: password policy check, 2FA enrollment status
  • Patch & update any remaining devices or software
 3 hrs
Day 6 — Saturday
  • Compile 2-week IT summary report for leadership
  • Update IT documentation (passwords vault, network diagrams, SOPs)
  • Plan upcoming projects & schedule next consultation cycle
 3.5 hrs
Week 2 Total 20 hrs
